Mr. Whiskerworth’s

A gourmet cat food brand that had to feel premium enough to justify the price, and warm enough to still feel like home.

Role
Lead Graphic Designer
Timeline
September 2024
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Procreate
Mr. Whiskerworth’s hero image

The Challenge

Mr. Whiskerworth's is a premium cat food brand built around a mascot with real personality: a monocle-wearing feline gentleman meant to carry the whole identity. The brief had a built-in tension: feel premium enough to justify a gourmet position, while staying warm and approachable enough that everyday pet owners still feel invited in.

The Solution

I built out a full mascot design, a refined gentleman cat in a tailored suit, and a color system that shifts across the packaging line to distinguish different age ranges while staying recognizably one brand.

Identity

The Mark

The logo does double duty by folding the character straight into the typography, so the mascot is present even where he isn't literally drawn.

Letterform detail: the K curves into a cat's tail, the O becomes a monocle — the character is built into the wordmark itself.

The Outcome

The final identity system includes logo variations, a complete color palette, the full mascot character design, and packaging visuals, positioned to read as a new standard for how premium pet food can look on a shelf.
