Mr. Whiskerworth’s

A gourmet cat food brand: premium enough to earn the price, warm enough to feel like home.

Role
Lead Graphic Designer
Timeline
September 2024
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Procreate

The Challenge

Mr. Whiskerworth’s is a premium cat food brand built from scratch: mascot, logo, and packaging. The brief held one tension. It had to feel gourmet, yet still welcome everyday pet owners.

The mascot settled it: a monocled gentleman who is refined, not stiff, and playful, not cartoonish.

Brand Foundation

Brand Goals

Mealtime should be an experience of both taste and nourishment. Mr. Whiskerworth is the brand’s voice, and the guidelines sum it up in one line: healthy bowls, licked clean.

01

Deliver premium nutrition

Fresh, high-quality ingredients, formulated for every life stage.

02

Build trust

Be open about sourcing, preparation, and what each ingredient does.

03

Make mealtime a delight

Flavors and textures that suit a cat’s natural tastes.

04

Lead with care

Sustainable sourcing, packaging, and delivery.

Brand Character

Meet Mr. Whiskerworth

Character sketch

A refined, dapper feline with a sharp monocle, a tailored suit, and a perfectly groomed tail.

But he is not just about looks. He is driven by a passion to make sure every cat, from playful kitten to distinguished elder, gets the highest quality meals. To him, cat food should be a gourmet experience, packed with nutrition and balanced for taste and health.

Character Sheet

One sheet so he can appear anywhere: six poses and scenes, plus twelve head expressions.

Packaging

The Product Line

Four flavors, each with its own color and mascot scene, across three life stages. Color marks the flavor. A small tab marks the life stage.

Flavors

  • Aristocrat’s Delight · Chicken & Beef
  • Majesty’s Reserve · Mutton & Fish
  • Highland Hunt · Chicken & Mutton
  • Admiral’s Choice · Chicken & Fish

Life stage tabs

  • Kitten · up to 6 months
  • Adult · 6 months to 10 years
  • Senior · 10 years and up

Key Takeaways

  • This project was about creating something premium: a pet brand where the logo, palette, mascot, and packaging all feel elevated and consistent.
  • I worked to make sure everything the brief demanded was fulfilled, and checked each deliverable against it.
  • The design is practical, scalable, and print-ready, built to work across all 12 packs, from four flavors and three life stages.
