Imagin8 V Park

What if we had the time to imagine, and the audacity to dream? A question posed by collaborators during ideation that shaped the whole project.

Role

Timeline

Imagin8 V Park hero image

The Challenge

Imagin8 V Park is a digital empathy engine for civic transformation, an attempt to make the wicked problem of the housing crisis legible to middle and high school students and the wider community, without flattening it into something simplistic. The goal was an accessible, interactive way into a policy issue that's usually discussed only in rooms most people never get into.

The Solution

The core of the experience is an interactive, scenario-based simulation built as a high-fidelity Figma prototype, letting users digitally visualize and customize community spaces while picking up civic and housing concepts along the way.

Narrative Design

The Story

01) Dystopian Present

Players start in a neighborhood in decline, the wicked problem made visible and personal.

02) Meet Cherry & Barry

Two guides pull players back in time to where the decline began.

03) Community Tasks

Players take on civic engagement tasks, learning housing concepts by doing.

04) Thriving Present

Players return to a present day neighborhood shaped directly by their decisions.

The Outcome

The result is a polished, fully interactive click-and-play prototype ready for partner handoff and deployment. Alongside it: a validated model checked against real-world precedents, an established deployment roadmap, and a clear list of next steps and potential partnerships to take it further.
