Flipped

A brand identity built to feel like fun the moment you walk through the door.

Role
Lead Graphic Designer
Timeline
2024 · Client: AA Joyland (Trampoline Park)
Tools
Adobe Illustrator, Procreate
Flipped hero image

The Challenge

Flipped is a full brand identity created for a trampoline park at AA Joyland — covering everything from logo and mascot design to signage and experiential design. The scope shifted mid-project when the original soft play area concept was eliminated for space and safety reasons, which meant re-planning under a tight deadline without letting the quality slip: a real test of creative problem-solving under pressure.

The Solution

The objective centered on an immersive, engaging brand identity that works across physical and experiential touchpoints — every decision, from logo through signage, aimed at delivering cohesive excitement the moment you walk in. The outcome balanced the client's vision with genuinely new ideas suited for real-world implementation.

The Outcome

The finished system delivered a recognizable, energetic brand presence in a competitive entertainment category, balancing the client's vision with genuinely new ideas, and left them with a complete brand system ready to implement across the space.
