The Challenge

Dolled Up is an immersive VR experience built in Unity, centered on a nostalgic, dreamy closet space inspired by the iconic wardrobe energy of Hannah Montana. Building it meant figuring out 3D modeling, scripting, and VR interaction design all at once, with no prior experience in any of the three.

The Solution

A nostalgic space modeled in Blender and built out in Unity, where users can explore a playful, curated environment inspired by early-2000s pop culture aesthetics. Every script, interaction, and most of the assets were built from scratch as part of the learning process itself — working through Unity fundamentals, Blender modeling, and VR-specific interaction patterns in parallel, letting the world grow more detailed as the technical skills caught up with the vision for it.

The Outcome

Currently an in-progress VR prototype, but already a full end-to-end learning journey: from zero Unity experience to a functional, explorable 3D environment. Planned next steps include more interactions, music, and general polish as the space keeps growing.

Reflection

This has been a full end-to-end learning journey, from zero Unity experience to a functional, explorable 3D environment, and that arc is still very much the point of the project.
